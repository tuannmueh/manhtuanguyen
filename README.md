## 👨‍🏫 About Me

I am **Dr. Manh Tuan Nguyen**, currently serving as the **Program Director of E-Commerce** at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH), Vietnam.

I hold a Ph.D. in Management Information Systems from the National Economics University (NEU), Vietnam. My academic background also includes two Master's degrees in Information Technology and Management Information Systems, and a Bachelor's degree in Information Technology.

With over **15 years of experience** in both academia and the software industry, I have worked at leading technology companies, including Global CyberSoft (now Hitachi Vantara Vietnam) and Harvey Nash, where I held key positions as Project Team Lead and System Solution Architect.

My work focuses on bridging academic research and practical technology applications, particularly in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Enterprise Architecture, and Digital Transformation.
